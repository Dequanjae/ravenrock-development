Technical Specifications

Built to Perform

Every RavenRock home is engineered for maximum efficiency, rapid deployment, and premium feel — while hitting top tiers of the BC Energy Step Code.

Factory-Built Precision

Every home is manufactured to CSA A277 standards in a controlled factory environment, guaranteeing consistent quality, rapid local permitting, and predictable timelines.

CleanBC Optimization

Engineered to max out CleanBC Better Homes rebates and federal funding by hitting top tiers of the BC Energy Step Code through vertical stacking and premium insulation.

Solar Integration

All roofs are fully composed of Building-Integrated Photovoltaics (BIPV) — seamless black solar tiles serving as the actual weathering roof layer, generating clean energy.

Cold-Climate Ready

Every unit includes a Tier-1 cold-climate ductless mini-split heat pump. Select lots integrate Level-2 EV charging stations directly at the driveway edge.

Modular Grid System

All 4 models share an identical 16-foot width profile, enabling plug-and-play assembly. Layout changes are possible even after civil underground utilities are trenched.

BC Building Code

Full compliance with BC Building Code and local permitting requirements. Factory-built to exact specifications for rapid on-site assembly and inspection.
