Our Story

Born from a belief that housing can be both affordable and extraordinary — RavenRock is redefining what home means in British Columbia.

Our Mission

To Make Sustainable Living Accessible to Every British Columbian

RavenRock Development Corp exists to solve one of BC's most pressing challenges: the housing affordability crisis. We believe that quality, eco-friendly housing shouldn't be a luxury — it should be a right.

Through innovative modular construction, thoughtful community design, and unwavering commitment to environmental stewardship, we create tiny home communities that prove affordability and excellence aren't mutually exclusive.

Our Values

Principles That Guide Us

Sustainability First

Every decision we make starts with the question: "Is this the most environmentally responsible path?" From materials to energy systems, we choose green without compromise.

Community Centred

Homes don't exist in isolation. We design entire communities — with shared spaces, green areas, and amenities that foster connection and belonging.

Innovative Design

We merge classical architectural principles with cutting-edge modular technology. The result: homes that are as beautiful as they are efficient.

Our Purpose

Articles of Incorporation

Benefit Statement

"RavenRock Development Corp. is dedicated to developing innovative, sustainable, and attainable compact mixed-use projects in British Columbia and across Canada that address housing need for low-income households, seniors, young families, and other underserved populations, creating long-term community value in partnership with local governments and community organizations."

Nature of Business

"Development and management of innovative compact mixed-use real estate projects across Canada, including land acquisition, project financing, residential and commercial strata and leasehold development, attainable and social housing initiatives, and sustainable community-focused real estate solutions serving individuals and families in housing need."

Meet the Vision Behind RavenRock

Our founder and CEO brings decades of experience in real estate development, sustainable construction, and community planning to RavenRock. Under this leadership, RavenRock has assembled a world-class team of architects, engineers, sustainability consultants, and community planners — all united by a shared vision of accessible, eco-friendly housing for all.
